**Name:** X Engagement List — top commenters & follow-back checker
**Short description (≤132 chars):** See who comments on your X posts most, which replies you haven't answered, and who doesn't follow back. Local only.

**Category:** Social & Communication · **Language:** English, 简体中文

## Description
X Engagement List turns what you already see on X into simple lists — without logging in to anything, without automation, and without sending your data anywhere.

**What it does**
- **Top commenters (7 / 30 / 90 days):** who replies to your posts most often, with a link to their latest reply.
- **To reply:** comments on your posts that you haven't answered yet.
- **Not following back:** open your own Following page, press "Start snapshot", and the extension scrolls slowly (about one screen every 2 seconds, stop any time) to list who doesn't follow you back.
- **Unfollowers:** compare two complete snapshots of your Followers page to see who left.
- **CSV export and multiple accounts** (Pro).

**How it works**
The extension reads the X pages you open in your own browser (your posts, replies, notifications, and your Following/Followers lists) and stores what it sees in your browser's local database. Nothing is uploaded. Records only grow as you browse — it never calls X's API and never browses for you, except the optional snapshot scroll on your own list that you start and can stop.

**What it never does**
No following, unfollowing, liking, replying, or messaging. No passwords, no X API keys, no tracking, no ads.

# Privacy Policy — X Engagement List / X 互动名单助手
_Last updated: 2026-10-22 · Publisher: ohomygood · Contact: go_with_prob@mail.grokbot.com_

## English
**What the extension reads.** On x.com only, the extension reads the page you are viewing: post IDs, author handles and display names, post text and timestamps, "Replying to" information, and — on your own Following/Followers pages — handles, names and the "Follows you" label. It also reads the handle of the signed-in account from the page navigation.

**Where it is stored.** Only in your browser's IndexedDB, inside the extension. It is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete one account's data in Settings, or all data by removing the extension.

**Network requests.** The extension makes one kind of request: when you activate or renew Pro, it sends your license key and a random device ID (generated by the extension, not linked to your identity) to our license server, which returns a signed token. The license server stores the key, its status, up to 3 device IDs, and the Stripe Checkout session/payment IDs needed to handle refunds. It does not store your name, e-mail or X account. The license server runs on Render; its records (license key, status, device IDs, payment references) also appear in the server's hosting logs so they survive restarts. Payments are processed by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy. When you click Upgrade, the Stripe checkout link carries the same random install ID as a reference (`client_reference_id`) so the license server can match your payment; it is stored with your license record.

**What we never do.** No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data, no automated actions on X, no collection of passwords, cookies or messages.

**Children.** Not directed to children under 13.

**Changes.** Material changes will be noted in the store listing and this page.

## 简体中文
**读取什么：** 仅在 x.com 上，读取你正在浏览的页面内容：帖子 ID、作者用户名与昵称、帖子文本与时间、「回复给」信息；在你自己的关注/粉丝页面读取用户名、昵称和「关注了你」标记；以及页面导航中当前登录账号的用户名。

**存在哪里：** 只存在你浏览器内本扩展的 IndexedDB 中，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。你可以在「设置」中删除某个账号的数据，或卸载扩展删除全部数据。

**网络请求：** 只有一种：激活或续期 Pro 时，把你的激活码和一个随机设备 ID（由扩展生成，与身份无关）发送到授权服务器，换取签名令牌。授权服务器只保存激活码、状态、最多 3 个设备 ID，以及处理退款所需的 Stripe 付款会话/支付 ID；不保存姓名、邮箱或 X 账号。授权服务器部署在 Render，上述授权记录也会写入服务器的托管日志，以便重启后可恢复。付款由 Stripe 处理，适用 Stripe 隐私政策。点击「购买 Pro」时，Stripe 付款链接会附带同一个随机安装 ID 作为订单参考号（client_reference_id），授权服务器用它匹配付款，并与授权记录一起保存。

**绝不会做：** 无统计、无追踪、无广告，不出售或共享数据，不在 X 上执行任何自动操作，不收集密码、Cookie 或私信。
